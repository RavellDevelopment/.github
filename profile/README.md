## ► Current project: Housing, rebuilt

In Housing, every player gets a house of their own! Build whatever you like, then wire it up with triggers and actions to turn it into a parkour, a hangout or even a full minigame.

It won't be a one-to-one copy of Hypixel's Housing, but we're building as much of it as we can, and building it properly.

| | |
|---|---|
| **01 · Claim** | Pick a theme and get a house of your own. |
| **02 · Build** | Shape it block by block, from a cozy cabin to a castle. |
| **03 · Wire** | Hook events to actions and make things happen. |
| **04 · Share** | Open the doors, welcome visitors and collect cookies. |

## ► What we're rebuilding

Here's a look at the parts of Housing we're working through. Some are further along than others!

- **Event actions:** joins, clicks, deaths, regions and parkour, each with its own list of actions.
- **NPCs & holograms:** characters and floating text that greet, guide and react to players.
- **Items & menus:** custom items and menus that run your actions when someone clicks them.
- **Stats & scoreboards:** count coins, kills or laps and show them live on the sidebar.
- **Groups & permissions:** decide who can build, who's a guest and who isn't welcome.
- **Visits & cookies:** browse houses, hop into a friend's and give cookies to the best ones.
